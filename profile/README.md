# Laqeeni (لقيني)

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Laqeeni**, the premier digital platform and trades directory connecting users with local traders, businesses, and professional services across **Saudi Arabia** 🇸🇦.

## 🌐 About Us
Laqeeni is built to streamline local commerce, making it simple for consumers to find trusted service providers, skilled technicians, and tradespeople in their immediate vicinity. We leverage modern technology to bridge the gap between traditional merchants and digital-first consumers.

Whether users are looking for certified professionals in **Riyadh**, **Jeddah**, or other major cities, our directory simplifies the process of hiring.

* **Official Website:** [Visit Laqeeni English](https://laqeeni.net)
* **Platform Homepage:** [Laqeeni Portal](https://laqeeni.net)
* **Contact Support:** hello@laqeeni.net

## 🛠️ Supported Local Trades & Services
Our platform maps out reliable local help across a comprehensive suite of industries, including:
* **Home Maintenance:** AC repair (فني تكييف), plumbing (سبّاك), electrical work (كهربائي), and painting (دهّان).
* **Logistics & Care:** Professional movers (نقل عفش) and home cleaning services (تنظيف).
* **Automotive:** On-demand car repair (صيانة سيارات) and roadside assistance.

## 🚀 Tech Stack & Engineering
We believe in building scalable, fast, and secure infrastructure to support local commerce frameworks and digital directory mapping. Keep an eye on this space as we release open-source tools, Arabic localization packages, and developer components tailored specifically for the Middle East market.
